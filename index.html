The problem

A multi-branch retailer keeps one cashbook workbook with a sheet per bank account: USD and ZiG accounts at several banks, mobile cash deposits and rand. At month end that can be 50 or more sheets and over 25,000 lines.

To answer simple questions, such as everything a branch banked across all accounts, or which lines still have no posting reference, someone had to open sheet after sheet. The sheets are not even laid out the same way: some have a Statement Ref column and some do not, the branch is typed into a red column only some of the time, and a few dates are typed as text.

How the query works

  1. Reads the cashbook path from one named cell, so next month only the path changes.
  2. Lists every sheet in the workbook and skips the non-bank ones (here Org and Bank charges & Imtt). A bank sheet added next month is picked up without touching the code.
  3. On each sheet, finds the “Opening balance” row. Whether it sits in the 3rd or 4th column tells the query which layout the sheet uses, and every column after it shifts by one to match.
  4. Keeps the opening line and every line with a date and an amount. Dates typed as text (1/9/2026 02:00:00) and amounts typed with commas are converted.
  5. Adds the bank sheet name, the currency read from that name, a Status (Opening, Posted, X or No ref) and a Net amount, and keeps each sheet's lines in their original order.
